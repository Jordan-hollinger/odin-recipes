Veggie Wellington

Home

Description

This is a vegitarian take on the classic beef wellington.

Ingredients

  • Premade vegitarian filling of your choice
  • 8 x 10 Puff pastry sheet

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425
  2. Lay puff pastry on oiled sheet pan
  3. Fill roughly half of the pastry with filling
  4. Fold pastry over on top and brush with egg wash or olive oil
  5. Bake 25 minutes or until golden on outside
